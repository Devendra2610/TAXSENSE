Automated CA-level Income Tax Return (ITR) audit and review engine with Form 26AS/AIS reconciliation, expense disallowances, regime comparison, and BS/P&amp;L mapping.
# ⚖️ TaxSense Review Portal — CA Tax Review & Audit Engine

> An intelligent tax review and audit automation platform for Chartered Accountants and tax professionals to streamline ITR verification, Form 26AS reconciliation, and tax compliance checks.

### 🚀 Key Capabilities:
- 📋 **ITR Form Validation & Suitability**: Pre-filing checks across ITR-1 to ITR-5.
- 🏷️ **Income Classification Engine**: Multi-head income segregation & anomaly detection.
- 🚫 **Expense Disallowance Engine**: Automated checks under Sec 40(a)(ia), 40A(3), 43B, and Sec 37.
- 🔄 **26AS & AIS Reconciliation**: Automated TDS credit matching and carry-forward validation (Rule 37BA).
- ⚖️ **Old vs. New Tax Regime Analysis**: Tax liability optimization u/s 115BAC & 87A.
- 📊 **Balance Sheet & P&L Mapping**: Financial statement mapping to Part A-BS / Part A-P&L schedules.
- 📑 **Audit Report Export**: Instant multi-sheet Excel audit report generation.
